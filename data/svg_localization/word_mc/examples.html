One option appears somewhere in the figure; the other three appear nowhere in it. The correct word is drawn only from labels that already passed the localization set's filters — legible at this resolution, not occluded, unique in the scene — so a word the model physically cannot read is never the answer. Every distractor is verified absent as a substring across all text in the scene, including the title, footnotes and badges.
Scoring. Exact letter match (multiple_choice). Chance is 25%.
These questions run on the existing scenes. No image was re-rendered to create them — they are derived from scenes.jsonl and point at the same PNGs and SVGs as the localization set. A model can therefore be scored on all three sets over identical pixels.
